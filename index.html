1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Prayer Times application (“the App”), provided by MTC Tech., accesses and uses information when you use the App. The current release is free and does not contain advertising or advertising SDKs.

2. Information and permissions used by the App

The App may request or use the following information and device capabilities when needed for features you choose to use:

  • Approximate and precise location: to determine prayer times, select or update a location, support Qibla/location-related features, display map content, and help open nearby-mosque searches. You may use manual location options where available instead of granting location access.
  • Notifications, alarms, wake lock, and boot completion: to schedule and deliver prayer notifications, reminders, and alarms, including time-sensitive alarm behavior.
  • Camera, images/media, microphone, and audio: only for optional features that require them and only after user action/permission. The App does not automatically upload these files to a server operated by MTC Tech..
  • Local app data: settings, language/theme preferences, selected/favorite locations, notes, alarms, reminders, and similar app state may be stored locally on your device.

3. Network and third-party services

Some optional features rely on external services. For example, map tiles may be requested from OpenStreetMap infrastructure, and a nearby-mosque action may open Google Maps or a browser with location-related parameters when you explicitly request that action. Geocoding/location lookup functionality may also use platform or third-party services.

When your device contacts an external service, that service may receive technical network information such as an IP address and the information necessary to fulfill the request. Those services operate under their own privacy terms.

4. User-initiated sharing

If you choose a Share function, the App passes the selected content to the Android sharing interface so that you can choose the destination app or service. MTC Tech. does not control the privacy practices of the destination you choose.

5. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The current release does not include ads, advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or a developer-operated user account/login system.

6. Retention and deletion

MTC Tech. does not operate a user-account database for this release. App settings and user-created app data are generally stored locally on the device and may remain there until you delete the relevant item, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Information processed by external services is subject to those services’ own retention rules.

Privacy or deletion-related questions may be sent to prayer.times.support@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical practices appropriate to the App’s design. However, no device, network transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when App features, data practices, third-party services, or legal/platform requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

9. Contact
